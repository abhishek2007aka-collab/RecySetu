# Real e-waste facts for India and Uttarakhand (for the PPT and report)

| Fact | Figure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| E-waste generated in India, 2024-25 | 13.97 lakh tonnes (12.54 lakh in 2023-24) | CPCB data in Rajya Sabha reply, reported by UNI: https://www.uniindia.com/news/parliament/par-e-waste-rsq/3542425.html |
| Uttarakhand, e-waste collected/processed | 1,34,255 t (2023-24); 1,13,562 t (2024-25) | Parliament reply, PIB: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2147876 (table column covers collection/dismantling/recycling; treat as processed) |
| State-wise generation data | Not available; generation is estimated nationally from sales and product life | Same PIB reply |
| Authorised e-waste recyclers/dismantlers in Uttarakhand | 8 units, total installed capacity 1,53,068 tonnes/year (Attero, Bhagwanpur alone 1,44,000) | CPCB list as on 08-06-2023: https://www.ndmc.gov.in/pdf/cpcb_approved_list_of_e-waste_recyclers_dismantler.pdf |
| Share of India's e-waste recycling done by the informal sector | about 90-95% (older estimates; formal share is rising) | ICRIER working paper: https://icrier.org/pdf/Working_Paper_429.pdf |
